# Business Analysis Portfolio

**Saish Dekate**

A collection of business analysis case studies covering the core BA lifecycle: requirement elicitation, process analysis, data analysis, and requirements validation through user stories and UAT.

Each project starts from a business scenario and produces the deliverables a Business Analyst would hand to stakeholders: a BRD, process flowcharts, an Excel analysis model, use cases and test cases.

## Projects

| # | Project | Business problem | Key deliverables | Tools |
|---|---------|------------------|------------------|-------|
| 01 | [Student Leave Management System – BRD](01-brd-student-leave-management/) | A college runs leave requests on paper; students can't track status and staff waste time searching forms | Business Requirements Document, stakeholder register, functional and non-functional requirements, communication plan, user stories | Requirements documentation, process modelling |
| 02 | [Order Fulfilment – AS-IS and TO-BE Process Analysis](02-process-analysis-order-fulfilment/) | An online retailer confirms stock, invoices and books couriers manually, causing delays and wrong dispatches | Swimlane flowcharts (AS-IS, TO-BE), issue/impact analysis, KPIs, risks and controls | Process mapping, BPMN-style swimlanes |
| 03 | [Sales and Product Profitability Analysis](03-sales-profitability-analysis/) | A retail manager needs to understand monthly sales and which products earn the most profit | Excel model with formulas and summary sheet, charts, one-page insight summary | Excel (SUMIFS, INDEX/MATCH, charts) |
| 04 | [Scheduled Food Delivery – User Stories, Use Case and UAT](04-user-stories-use-case-uat/) | A food-delivery app adds "schedule for later" ordering with delivery slots | User stories, acceptance criteria, detailed use case, UAT test case sheet with edge cases | Agile requirements, UAT planning, Excel |

## Skills demonstrated

- **Requirement elicitation:** stakeholder identification, interviews, workshops, surveys, observation
- **Requirements documentation:** BRD, functional / non-functional requirements, MoSCoW prioritisation, scope, assumptions and constraints
- **Process analysis:** AS-IS / TO-BE swimlane mapping, root-cause and impact analysis, process improvement
- **Data analysis:** revenue and profitability modelling, KPI definition, data visualisation, business insights
- **Agile and validation:** user stories, Given / When / Then acceptance criteria, use cases, UAT test design, traceability
